About

I'm a mechanical engineering student who ended up doing ML, mostly the kind that tries to explain why something happened. A lot of my work ends up as a graph: how a server fails, how fraudsters share devices, how a spider decides what to spin next.

I like taking an idea from a pitch to a working demo. Lighthouse began as something I kept raising in standups, and the fraud prototype ended with an end to end demo in front of the client.

Tools I reach for most: Python, PyTorch, LightGBM, scikit-learn, LangGraph, FastAPI, and TypeScript with Next.js and Express on the product side. Postgres with pgvector when something needs search.

Outside work I'm into cars and gadgets.
